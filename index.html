Key findings (full dataset)

  • Scale: ₹1.50 crore in sales (₹14.96M) and ₹3.75M profit across 9,994 orders, a 25.1% profit margin.
  • Growth: sales rose every year, from ₹2.98M in 2015 to ₹4.98M in 2018 (+67%). Sep, Nov and Dec are the strongest months.
  • Categories: Eggs, Meat & Fish (15.2%) and Snacks (15.0%) lead, but the seven categories sit within 13.6–15.2% of sales, so the mix is balanced. Oil & Masala has the lowest margin (24.4%).
  • Sub-categories: Health Drinks (₹1.05M) and Soft Drinks (₹1.03M) are clear leaders. Spices, Atta & Flour and Chicken have the lowest margins (~23.8–23.9%) and are the top candidates for pricing review; Noodles and Fish have the highest (26.3%).
  • Regions: West (32%) and East (28%) generate the most sales and profit; South has the highest margin among the larger regions (25.6%).
  • Discount: there is no relationship between discount and profit (correlation 0.00). Margin stays near 25% from 10% to 35% discounts, so deeper discounts do not appear to cost or add profit in this data.
  • Geography: all orders are in Tamil Nadu. Kanyakumari, Vellore and Bodi lead on sales; Kanyakumari (24.4%) and Tenkasi (24.3%) have among the weakest margins.

Data notes: dates arrive in two formats (dd-mm-yyyy and m/d/yyyy) and were standardised. The North region has a single order, so it is not meaningful for comparison. The dataset has no product-name column, so sub-category is used as the product level.
